# Brand assets

## Source assets

- `laiaudio/data/paolium-logo.svg`: the Paolium wheat logo, used as the app icon and in the footer. It is copied
  from the Paolium brand repository and must not be edited here.

## Identity

- Product name: **Laiaudio**. It reads the same in English and Spanish ("audio"), matching a bilingual app.
- Laiaudio is a Paolium product: the footer reads "Designed by Paolium." and links to `https://paolium.web.app/`.
- Tagline: "Live captions for your meetings, powered by AI that never leaves your computer."

## Rules

- Use the palette in `docs/design.md`; do not introduce new accent colors.
- Keep the gold dot after "Paolium" in brand text.
- Do not add emojis to the UI, the documentation headings or commit messages.
